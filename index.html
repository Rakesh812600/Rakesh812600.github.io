01 / ABOUT

Research with purpose.

I am a researcher affiliated with CSIR-CEERI, Pilani, India, working in computer vision, machine learning, and video understanding. My interests include weakly supervised video anomaly detection, temporal representation learning, and multimodal reasoning for surveillance and safety-related applications.

My research aims to build models that are robust, interpretable, efficient, and grounded in carefully evaluated evidence. I am especially interested in learning temporal patterns from limited labels and combining visual observations with contextual knowledge.

FEATURED RESEARCH / TRAN SIGHT

TranSight: architecture & results.

Unsupervised video anomaly detection for intelligent transportation surveillance · IEEE APSCON 2026.

Research overview

TranSight addresses unusual activity detection in transportation surveillance under limited labeled data. It combines a multi-scale Wider-ResNet encoder, a bidirectional temporal shift module (Bi-TSM), a Temporal Transformer, and dual-attention refinement using residual channel attention blocks (RCABs) and spatial-temporal attention (STA). The system uses reconstruction-based anomaly scoring, supplemented by gradient, structural, perceptual, and contrastive training objectives.
